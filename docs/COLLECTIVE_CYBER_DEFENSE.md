# XUNIA Collective Cyber Defense Ontology

Command: **`/glass collective defense`**

This is an official XUNIA upgrade derived from OpenAI's public call for collective cyber defense and its complete 128-signatory roster.

Sources:

- [OpenAI article and official roster](https://openai.com/collective-cyberdefense/)
- [OpenAI source post](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2093074192636018977)
- User-provided screenshot dated August 27, 2026

## Claim boundary

Every listed organization is represented as a public signatory reference. The ontology does not claim that XUNIA is a signatory, partner, customer, certified implementation, or endorsed project. It does not infer any organization's proprietary internal standard.

Alignment uses four evidence states:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `TARGET` | XUNIA intends to address the control. |
| `IMPLEMENTED` | A scoped implementation exists and awaits evidence review. |
| `EVIDENCED` | Current artifacts demonstrate operation within a stated scope and period. |
| `EXTERNALLY_ATTESTED` | An identified independent reviewer has issued a scoped attestation. |

Promotion beyond `TARGET` requires evidence and review. External attestation requires a specific attestation reference.

## Collective baseline

The ontology converts the public call into 15 control families:

1. Leadership priority
2. Highest-risk remediation
3. Least privilege
4. Strong authentication and access control
5. Defense in depth and compensating controls
6. Secure purchased, built, deployed, and AI-generated software
7. Continuous defense testing
8. Verified fixes
9. Threat-intelligence and tested-playbook sharing
10. Incident containment, response, and recovery
11. Critical-infrastructure support
12. Traceable and accountable agent identity
13. Observability and continuous monitoring
14. Authorized testing and private disclosure
15. Measurable protection, containment, and fix outcomes

## Evidence rule

Each promoted control requires an owner, implementation reference, test result, observation timestamp, scope, limitations, reviewer, and—when applicable—an external attestation reference. A logo, signatory listing, source-code assertion, or self-description alone is not compliance evidence.

## Decision policy

Public-source reads, peer registration, control mapping, target-state assessment, evidence attachment, and gap reporting are allowed. Implementation, evidence, attestation, publication, and remediation claims require review. Endorsement, partnership, certification, proprietary-standard inference, evidence-free promotion, authorization bypass, and offensive automation are blocked.
